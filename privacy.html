Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Omnipulse Labs handles user information for the Thermal Stress Calculator application.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Thermal Stress Calculator runs locally on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information, location data, or device telemetry.

2. Calculation Data

All structural inputs, material properties, thermal calculations, and generated PDF reports are processed entirely on your local device. No engineering data is transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding this policy, contact us through the developer distribution page on Google Play.
